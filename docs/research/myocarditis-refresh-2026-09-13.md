  a living label does not prove a newer update. Full risk-of-bias work,

  prove equivalence. Full methods, records and disclosures were not appraised.

  Self-controlled comparisons and the young-male subgroup are distinguished
  from a complete benefit-risk calculation. Original PMC access failed and
  the official full-text XML endpoint returned 404; no unavailable full methods,

- [CDC June 2025 monitoring slides](https://www.cdc.gov/acip/downloads/slides-2025-06-25-26/04-Meyer-COVID-508.pdf):
  original slide 24 visually checked. Provider-assessed recovery concerns
  surveyed cases from 2021; the one-year evidence is explicitly unpublished.
  It is not a new independent cohort or proof of uniformly normal MRI.
- [FDA June 25, 2025 communication](https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/safety-availability-biologics/fda-approves-required-updated-warning-labeling-mrna-covid-19-vaccines-regarding-myocarditis-and):

  a specific formulation and short window; its separate imaging discussion
  concerns older-formula cases. Underlying analyses were not independently
  reappraised, and the communication is not a new 2026 clinical study.
- [Hviid 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38365960/),
  DOI `10.1093/eurheartj/ehae056`: fetched Consensus record followed by the
  original author abstract. The comparison is with the later post-dose-two
  period, not placebo. Full publisher retrieval failed; complete calendar dates,
  adjustment methods, figures, supplements and disclosures were not appraised.
- [Semenzato 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11348078/),
  DOI `10.1001/jama.2024.16380`: selected original design, results,
  limitations and disclosures assessed. The comparison concerns prognosis
  among hospitalized cases, not population incidence or a causal benefit from
  vaccination. Not every limitation, supplement or patient record was appraised;

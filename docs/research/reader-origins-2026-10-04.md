## Novelty and scope

The current source baseline is 804 reviews and 18 guides. The human-origins
topic has ten reviews. A catalog-wide title/slug check excludes the existing
living-chimpanzee ancestry, evolutionary-ladder, African-origin, archaic
interbreeding, Neanderthal-culture and dinosaur-transition questions.

The new questions address locomotion before major brain enlargement, dating
clock applicability, restricted mitochondrial ancestry, regional ancient diets
and the preservation limits of ancient DNA. These are coverage-audit gaps, not
measured reader requests or evidence of visitor demand. No private feedback or
frozen usefulness questions were used to select them.

The material is educational evidence interpretation. It includes no laboratory
procedures, biological engineering, medical diet recommendation, wild-food
instruction or claim about recreating extinct organisms. The initially
considered Neanderthal-disappearance and dinosaur-colour questions are not
part of this tranche and do not count as researched, written or published.

## Actual research

Three known Consensus searches and three record fetches were used in this
tranche, with zero Deep Reviews. The fetched records are Gunz et al. 2020,
Kabukcu et al. 2022 and Moubtahij et al. 2024. Quota remaining is unavailable.
Connector summaries were discovery aids; public citations point to originals.

- Gunz et al., DOI `10.1126/sciadv.aaz4729`: original abstract, discussion
  and disclosures inspected through the Europe PMC full-text XML archive.
  The research browser encountered a challenge on the PMC page; archive access
  succeeded. No reconstructed brain volume was independently recalculated.
- IntCal20, DOI `10.1017/RDC.2020.41`: primary calibration scope,
  reference-data integration and uncertainty discussion inspected. The
  55,000-calendar-year endpoint is not a universal sample-dating guarantee.
- Poznik et al., DOI `10.1126/science.1237619`: primary inheritance
  definitions, sampling, calibration dependence and acknowledgements inspected.
  No historical coalescence date is advertised as a fixed current estimate.
- Rohde et al., DOI `10.1038/nature02842`: primary abstract, model scope
  and disclosures inspected. Full models and supplements were not independently
  audited, and model dates are not adopted as observed historical dates.
- Piperno et al., DOI `10.1038/nature02734`: primary abstract, archaeological
  context and disclosures inspected. Subscription body was not fully reviewed.
- Kabukcu et al., DOI `10.15184/aqy.2022.143`: publisher PDF checked for
  regional charred plant evidence, interpretation limits and funding. Online
  year 2022 differs from the 2023 journal issue. An HTML request returned 429;
  the original PDF remained available. No food-processing recipe is extracted.
- Moubtahij et al., DOI `10.1038/s41559-024-02382-z`: publisher PDF checked
  for multi-isotope findings, local context, animal-food qualification and
  disclosures. Plant reliance is not exclusive vegetarianism or global calories.
- Van der Valk et al., DOI `10.1038/s41586-021-03224-9`: primary abstract,
  specimen context and disclosures inspected. Subscription methods were not
  fully reviewed; no claim of full-methods appraisal or current oldest record.
- Kjær et al., DOI `10.1038/s41586-022-05453-y`: primary geological age
  discussion, preservation interpretation and disclosures inspected, including
  acknowledged Illumina collaboration. Sediment fragments are not whole genomes
  of every organism, and preservation differs from bone.

Smithsonian walking, brain and dating explanations and the USGS radiometric
timescale explanation were checked as institutional method context, not

but not used: archive retrieval failed, and a bone-decay estimate must not be
generalized to a universal DNA half-life.

bounded identity/linked-notice check, not exhaustive retraction clearance.

## Simplifications not carried into the review

- NOAA's simple energy-not-water wave phrase is not a zero-transport claim:
  Stokes drift, currents and breaking remain explicit.
- The salt summary's erroneous magma-from-Earth's-core wording is not adopted.
  The original crust-water fact sheet describes additions, removals and sinks.
- NASA's simplified PSU-to-mass wording is not used as a unit definition.
- Artesian head is not explained by literal rock weight alone, and a deep well
  is not automatically artesian or self-flowing.
- Conceptual groundwater travel times, historical volume tables, station totals,
  mission forecasts and illustrated layer depths are not current universal data.
- Tide height, current speed, range, phase, surge and total water level are
  separate quantities; none supplies a generic local safety rule.

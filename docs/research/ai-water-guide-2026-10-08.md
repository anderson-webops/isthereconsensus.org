## Scope and novelty

`/guides/reading-ai-water-footprints` adds a substantial standalone explanation
of a genuine coverage gap: interpreting AI and data-center water claims.
Related electricity reviews are explicitly background, not AI-water answers.
This is one new reading guide, not a new reviewed-claim row, approved reader
submission, expert appraisal or publication receipt. The canonical source
library remains 1,001 claims; the original 201-review proposal is unchanged.

The external question audit is now exposed development material. Its original
classification and first result remain frozen. This addition cannot be used to
reclassify that sample as fresh coverage or a successful search evaluation.
Public deployment, full guide readback and the broader goal remain separate.

## Original-source checks

Seven paragraph-cited references retain distinct evidence roles:

- [USGS glossary](https://www.usgs.gov/water-science-school/science/water-science-glossary): technical definitions, not site-impact evidence.
- [Berkeley Lab 2024 report](https://doi.org/10.71468/P1WC7Q): selected operational methods and historical estimates, not AI metering.
- [Patterns perspective](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patter.2025.101430): original reporting limitations; first online December 17, 2025, despite its 2026 journal issue.
- [Berkeley Lab 2025 Update](https://www.energy.gov/documents/united-states-data-center-energy-usage-report-2025-update): original report published June 18, 2026; selected scenario and equipment methods, not a water inventory.
- [IEA 2026 assessment](https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai/executive-summary): selected task and aggregate-demand interpretation, not a per-request water measurement.
- [Siddik, Shehabi and Marston](https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/abfba1): selected spatial methods and trade-offs from original author-repository text, not updated local evidence.
- [USGS water-use overview](https://www.usgs.gov/mission-areas/water-resources/science/water-use-united-states): supply categories and estimation scope.

Selected sections were read, not every appendix, supplement or underlying
dataset. Agency pages and journal records are not independent replications of
the same results. No percentage of scientists agreeing, named facility verdict,
precise personal footprint or universal bottle-per-query number is invented.
The arithmetic illustration is explicitly independently constructed and
hypothetical. The checklist is editorial synthesis, not a validated instrument.

The accessible original 2024 PDF was read through the research tool; a separate
local download returned HTTP 403 and was not bypassed. Publisher and PMC access
to the perspective was unreliable; its original full-text XML was retrieved
from Europe PMC. Metadata, AI allocation, water boundaries, reporting and the
declared competing-interest statement were inspected. Declaration of no
competing interests is self-reported, not an independent verification. No
exhaustive correction/retraction or funding audit is claimed.

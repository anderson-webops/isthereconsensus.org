## Original evidence and interpretation

- [USPSTF 2022 recommendation](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/vitamin-supplementation-to-prevent-cvd-and-cancer-preventive-medication):
  original recommendations and selected rationale read. Its I statements concern
  an unresolved benefit/harm balance for CVD/cancer prevention, not every health
  outcome. D recommendations concern beta carotene and vitamin E for those
  purposes. Known deficiency, pregnancy and illness fall outside this prevention
  scope. The [NIH consumer page](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/WYNTK-Consumer/)
  supplies context on indications, ingredients and interactions; its original
  effectiveness and safety sections were read.
- [O'Connor 2022 evidence report](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/document/final-evidence-summary/vitamin-supplementation-to-prevent-cvd-and-cancer-preventive-medication),
  DOI `10.1001/jama.2021.15650`: original methods, selected pooled results,
  limitations and funding read. Searches through February 1, 2022; English-language
  trials in predominantly healthy populations, with observational evidence for
  serious harms. Extraction was checked by a second reviewer, and study quality
  independently assessed by two reviewers. Rare-event pooling methods varied by
  event frequency. A small pooled cancer-incidence benefit is retained; few large
  trials, variable formulations and multiple analyses limit inference. COSMOS is
  already included, so its participants are not added again to the pooled total.
- [Sesso 2022 COSMOS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9170475/): original
  design, blinded adjudication, main results, discussion and disclosures read.
  Randomized factorial trial of 21,442 older adults, after an adherence run-in;
  median treatment 3.6 years. Invasive cancer, CVD and mortality estimates did not
  establish reductions. The favorable secondary lung-cancer result is retained
  with its unadjusted multiple-comparison limitation. A self-reported gastrointestinal
  bleeding signal (HR 1.17, 95% CI 1.03 to 1.32) is also retained and qualified.
  Duration is short for some
  cancer effects, and volunteer selection and formulation limit generalization.
- [Vyas 2024 cognition](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11103094/): original
  design, testing, statistical methods, results, attrition and disclosures read.
  Clinic analyzed 573 baseline-complete participants; 492 completed two-year
  testing. The primary Clinic global estimate included no difference; secondary
  memory favored supplementation. Pooled global cognition combines Clinic and
  Mind (2,731), while episodic memory also includes Web (5,203). Participant
  overlap was removed, but these remain substudies of one parent trial. Main
  models assume missing at random; secondary/subgroup analyses lacked multiplicity
  control. Small standardized score changes are not observed dementia delays.
  The paper was accepted in 2023 but published in its 2024 issue.
- [Sachs 2023 COSMOS-Mind](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10562510/): original
  telephone assessment, blinded panel adjudication, incidence table, limitations
  and disclosures read. Of 2,262 enrollees, 2,082 had a follow-up. Baseline cases
  were excluded from their respective incidence analyses. MCI and probable-dementia
  estimates were imprecise, with 110 and 14 events. The absence of statistical
  significance does not rule out benefit or harm. Cognitive trajectories among
  converters are exploratory and conditioned on a post-randomization outcome.
- [Li 2026 epigenetic clocks](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04239-3):
  publisher abstract, disclosures, funding and an extended-data caption read.
  The 958-person ancillary analysis reports small favorable two-year signals in
  two clocks, with uncertain clinical relevance. Main methods/results were behind
  subscription access. Main-outcome multiplicity handling was not assessed; an
  available subgroup caption explicitly describes no multiplicity adjustment.
  Neither longevity nor generalized rejuvenation is established here.
- [Li 2026 COVID analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42385272/): original
  PubMed abstract and conflict statement read. Publisher full text returned 403.
  Secondary analysis of 2020 outcomes in participants retained and adherent at
  January 2020; main infection estimate included no difference. Symptom findings
  rely on 338 cases, and the stronger per-protocol result needs its selection
  qualification. Full ascertainment, missing-data and adjustment methods were
  not appraised. This does not establish general immune protection.

Full supplements, visual figures and raw data were not independently appraised.
The 2026 reports have materially narrower access than the earlier original papers;
their citation notes and outcome limitations say so.

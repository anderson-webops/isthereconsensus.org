## Interpretation safeguards

Percentile and percentage-correct denominators remain distinct. A mean of
ranks is not silently converted to a rank of a mean, and score growth does
not imply rank growth. Grade equivalents are not curriculum mastery or overall
developmental age. Equating is not ordinary endpoint arithmetic, an individual
prediction or a causal effect. Reliable measurement does not support every use.
Classical true score is model-defined, not directly observed pure ability.
Error bands, cut points, ceiling effects and classification reliability retain
their assumptions; no universal interval or individual decision is supplied.

Raw item gaps, conditional DIF and reviewed bias are distinct. Absence of a
detected flag is not exhaustive fairness clearance. Construct-preserving access
and construct-changing modification are not automatically equivalent or
invalid. NAEP group estimation is not a personal diagnostic report. Statistical
significance is neither practical educational importance nor identified school
causality. NAEP Proficient is not an interchangeable state grade-level label.
Numerical counterexamples are independently invented, not real student data.

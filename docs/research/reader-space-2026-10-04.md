Prepared October 4, 2026, America/New_York; source-check cutoff
`2026-10-05T00:03:21.338Z` (October 5 UTC). The guide uses the UTC check date.
This batch adds ten genuinely new canonical reviews to the 809-review source
catalog and one substantial paragraph-cited guide. These are audited gaps, not
invented reader requests. No private feedback or visitor analytics were read.

## Novelty and scope

The full source catalog was checked before writing. None of the ten questions
already has a canonical review. Related older questions stay intact:

| New question | Distinct from existing coverage | Necessary qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orbital weightlessness | Moon landing and solar storms | Shared free fall, not absent gravity; residual accelerations vary |
| Lunar phases | Far-side daylight | Ordinary viewing geometry versus an eclipse shadow |
| Apparent retrograde motion | Astrology performance | Moving observer, not orbital reversal or behavioral effect |
| Galaxy lookback | Expansion and cosmic horizons | Emission versus arrival time; distinguish distance definitions |
| Webb display colors | Existing telescope discoveries | Measured filtered signal, mapped display, illustration distinction |
| Transit mass and composition | Established exoplanet existence | Radius, dynamical mass, density and interior models are separate |
| Conditional habitable zones | Confirmed extraterrestrial life | Modeled surface-water possibilities are not verified environments |
| Multiple lens images | Black holes and dark-matter particles | One source, several paths; require source identification and a lens model |
| Dark-energy naming | Expansion, Hubble tension and dark matter | Observed behavior does not identify a physical cause |
| Stellar brightness and distance | Stellar evolution | Apparent flux is not intrinsic luminosity or a distance ranking |

These are interpretive questions relevant to familiar space headlines. They
are not new discoveries, claims of current record-holders or a replacement for
the entire astronomy topic. Dedicated browse memberships and a connected guide
make the added material reachable without splitting one answer into duplicates.

## Research and access

Two Consensus searches were used: an initial broad exoplanet query and a
narrower habitability/transit query. Two selected results were fetched before
use: Kopparapu et al. (2014) and Luque and Palle (2022). No Deep Review was used;
remaining account quota was unavailable. Discovery is not treated as appraisal.

Original institutional NASA/ESA pages and author manuscripts were checked.
The source module and guide link directly to those originals, not to Consensus
discovery pages. General laws and measurement distinctions are used without
repeating dated discovery counts, mission forecasts or unsourced percentages
of researchers agreeing.

- Kopparapu et al., **10.1088/2041-8205/787/2/L29**: original PDF's model
  assumptions, conditional boundaries, limitations and acknowledgements checked.
  The cloud-free one-dimensional model is historical, not a universal present
  boundary or proof of any observed planet's surface water. NASA/Penn State/ERC
  support is disclosed. Its author manuscript and journal paper are one work.
- Luque and Palle, **10.1126/science.abl7164**: original manuscript's mass-plus-
  radius requirement, selected M-dwarf population, compositional interpretation
  and disclosures checked. Spanish, university and EU support and declared
  competing-interest statement are retained. A fitted water-rich interior is
  not an observed ocean. Supplements and raw observations were not reanalyzed.
- Kelly et al., **10.3847/2041-8205/819/1/L8**: original author manuscript's
  multi-path interpretation, predicted later image and acknowledgements checked.
  HST/STScI, NASA/NSF, fellowship and foundation support is disclosed. Repeated
  images describe one supernova, not independent explosions or direct particle
  detection. No lens model was independently fitted. One university PDF mirror
  failed in the browser; the original arXiv manuscript was available.
- Riess et al., **10.1086/300499**: original abstract and identity checked for
  historical distance-redshift context. Full analysis and acknowledgements were
  not audited. No modern parameter estimate, claim of absent conflicts or direct
  dark-energy substance identification is inferred.
- DESI's March 19, 2025 collaboration update is used as a **dated report of
  hints**, not an exhaustive assessment of newer analyses or a decision that
  evolving dark energy is established. Current model competition, combined
  significance and final-fate predictions are expressly outside the review.
- NASA and ESA pages explain mission observations and methods. Multiple pages
  from the same agency or shared observatory are not independent replications.
  Archived Goddard retrograde material and historical Gaia pages are used only
  for the stable measurement principles, not current operational statistics.
- ESA/Webb's image-processing example appears to contain a wavelength-unit
  typo; that number is not adopted. The corroborated general filter/display
  explanation is retained without copying the faulty numerical example.

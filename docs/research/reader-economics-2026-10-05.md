## Interpretation safeguards

Price levels, inflation rates and accumulated changes remain distinct. Household
fixed-basket counterexamples do not claim to reproduce the CPI estimator.
Shelter services are not asset purchases or every mortgage installment. Constant
rebasing preserves percentage changes but cannot make different baskets or local
index levels comparisons of absolute living costs. Exact real-pay ratios retain
hours, composition, basket and period limits. Seasonal status and vintage do not
resolve every irregular shock or establish integrity automatically.

Headline unemployment uses a labor-force denominator. Temporary-layoff exceptions
are retained. Participation exits can lower the rate without employment growth;
the counterexample does not establish that any actual decline occurred this way.
Jobs and people are different units. U-6 has its own enlarged denominator and
defined economic-part-time and marginal-attachment categories, not every desire
for additional work. Survey design explanations do not prove the magnitude or
cause of an actual discrepancy, or provide exhaustive clearance.

Nominal and real output use different price treatment; CPI is not automatically
a GDP deflator. Quarterly annualization compounds a rate, not a forecast or an
observed full year. Final output/value added avoids repeated intermediate sales.
Import subtraction removes foreign content, not a causal policy effect. The
independent one-link Fisher example verifies nonadditivity, with the reference-
period exception and valid growth-contribution measures retained. GDP per capita
is not median disposable income or complete welfare. ICP PPP conversions are not
cash quotations, equilibrium currency valuations or validated trading forecasts.

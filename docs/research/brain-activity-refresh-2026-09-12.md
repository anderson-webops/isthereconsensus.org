## Primary sources and access

- [Raichle 2001](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC14647/): original abstract,
  cohort methods, selected results/discussion and funding read. Two resting groups
  of 19 and an 11-person fixation comparison were separate analyses. Oxygen
  extraction defines a physiological baseline, not a fraction of unused tissue.
  Full tables, visual figures and raw data were not independently appraised.
- [Shokri-Kojori 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6370887/): original
  abstract, selected acquisition methods, results, metric definitions, limitations
  and disclosures read. Normalized relative measures describe network differences;
  they are not physical power, neuronal firing percentages or validated clinical
  diagnoses. Full supplements and visual figures were not appraised.
- [Volpi 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11342718/): original abstract,
  selected cohort/modeling methods, results, limitations and disclosures read.
  Feature selection used 26 training participants, with a separate 33-person
  testing dataset. Group-average and pooled individual model performance differ;
  they must not be interchanged. Modeling is correlational, with indirect and
  semiquantitative measurements. Full supplements and visual plots not appraised.
- [Volpi 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12081390/): original abstract,
  selected participant/kinetic methods and results, limitations and disclosures
  read. The 47-person follow-up uses the same research program as the 2024 testing
  cohort; exact participant overlap is unresolved. Explained variation is not
  tissue utilization. Non-simultaneous imaging, limited resolution and systemic
  influences on BOLD constrain interpretation. No independent supplement, figure
  or data appraisal.
- [Dekker 2012](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2012.00429/full):
  selected original recruitment methods and result-table text read during this
  batch. A volunteer survey from selected regions, not a neural activity experiment
  or a current worldwide belief estimate. Full questionnaire/disclosures unassessed.

The retained [Scientific American 2008 explanation](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/do-people-only-use-10-percent-of-their-brains/)
is historical journalism. Its original body was read, but imprecise momentary-use,
cell-ratio and knowledge-percentage statements are not used as quantitative
scientific evidence. Primary measurements support the revised explanation.

Raichle reports NIH/Dana Foundation support. Shokri-Kojori reports NIH/NIAAA
intramural support. Volpi reports NIH/NIA funding, with McDonnell Center support
also reported in 2024. The latter three papers report no competing interests.
Other disclosures were not fully assessed. No independent conflicts audit or
formal risk-of-bias assessment is claimed. High overall certainty is an editorial
judgment about the broad unused-reserve claim, not a formal grade for each model.

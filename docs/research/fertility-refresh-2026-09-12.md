## Primary evidence and access

- [National Academies 2024 chapter](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK607379/):
  original scope, selected mechanistic discussion and conclusions read. It favors
  rejection of causal female-infertility relationships for BNT162b2 and mRNA-1273,
  but finds evidence inadequate for Ad26.COV2.S and NVX-CoV2373. Its conclusions
  cannot be made equally certain across products or extended to every reproductive
  endpoint. This is not a new assessment by that panel of the later 2025 cohorts.
  Full committee appraisal and disclosures were not independently reassessed.
- [Wesselink 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8807200/): original
  recruitment, time-varying exposures, models, selected results, missing-data
  approach, limitations and disclosures read. Analyses include 2,126 couples,
  with male vaccination data for 1,369; many partner exposures were reported by
  the female participant. Left truncation was addressed and confounders adjusted
  using regression and propensity methods. Twenty imputed datasets addressed
  low covariate missingness. Eighty-two percent completed a follow-up; three
  percent of those were later lost. The sample was mostly White and highly
  educated. Observed conception attempts averaged 3.5 months after vaccination,
  not lifelong follow-up. Male-infection findings were imprecise. Full result
  tables, visual figures and supplements were not independently appraised.
- [Zace 2022 synthesis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9464596/): original
  selection, extraction, pooling, quality methods, selected results and limitations
  read. Twenty-nine studies, twenty pooled, with follow-up from seven days to nine
  months. The authors rated most studies moderate or poor using checklist-based
  criteria; these are not independent site appraisals. Mixed markers, clinical
  outcomes and small studies constrain inference. Pooled group means should not
  be mistaken for adjusted individual causal comparisons. The report does not
  establish that every individual marker was unchanged. Full figures, supplements
  and raw data were not independently appraised.
- [Chamani synthesis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37441788/): original PubMed
  abstract and financial disclosures read. Online July 2023, February 2024 issue;
  its search ended January 11, 2023. Twenty-five studies included nineteen with
  embryo-transfer outcomes and eighteen with stimulation outcomes; those samples
  overlap. Pooled clinical-pregnancy and live-birth estimates included no difference,
  with a less precise live-birth interval. Null tests do not establish equivalence.
  Full synthesis methods, supplements and visual figures remain unappraised.
- [Liu 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12240746/): original recruitment,
  exclusions, exposures, outcome definitions, adjusted models, selected main
  results, limitations and disclosures read. A single-center prospective Chinese
  cohort of 2,733 women undergoing their first frozen transfer found lower
  clinical-pregnancy and live-birth rates after inactivated vaccination. The signal
  was stronger with vaccination before ovarian stimulation. Selection excluded
  incomplete follow-up, missing information, infection and vaccination after
  transfer. Propensity-score adjustment includes treatment and embryo features
  potentially downstream of exposure; that complicates causal interpretation.
  The authors acknowledge unmeasured confounding and platform/generalization
  limits. This is not evidence of permanent infertility, an mRNA effect or an
  established ninety-day treatment rule. Full supplements, visual figures and
  raw data were not independently appraised.
- [Wei 2025](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/endocrinology/articles/10.3389/fendo.2025.1491259/full):
  original eligibility, vaccine records, statistical methods, selected outcome
  tables, limitations and disclosures read. Of 1,258 enrollees, fresh-transfer
  outcomes were reported for 475 vaccinated and 312 unvaccinated women. Live
  births were 220 and 139 respectively. Comparisons used ANOVA/chi-square/Fisher
  methods rather than adjusted regression. Fresh transfer is a different setting
  from Liu's frozen-transfer cohort; the studies are not exact replications. Small
  subgroups and selected transfers limit inference. Enrollment began January
  2021, while registration occurred January 2022. Full figures, supplements and
  raw data were not independently appraised.

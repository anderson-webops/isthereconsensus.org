# Weather quantities, forecasts and observation boundaries

Original-reference check cutoff: 2026-10-05T16:44:10.000Z.

## Coverage and distinct additions

The 947-review source catalog was searched by title, bottom line, stable core
and editorial summary for weather/forecast/humidity/dew/frost/exposure,
precipitation, remote-observation and pressure/front concepts. Neighboring
climate trends, cold-snap inference, flooding and physical-pressure content
were checked rather than reclassified. Eighteen genuinely new questions cover
rain-event probability versus duration and amount; temperature-dependent RH;
equal-RH moisture comparison; saturation versus rain; dew formation; frost
versus observation height; wind-chill and heat-index assumptions; snow density
and above-freezing arrival; sleet versus freezing rain; warm-surface hail;
radar sampling and target identity; satellite radiance versus surface air;
station/sea-level pressure; and three-dimensional fronts versus map traces.

These are audit-selected gaps, not invented reader submissions. Each body
contains 276-295 original words excluding shared metadata. Five deliberate
collections and a substantial original cited guide organize them; neither
collections nor guides increase the claim count. Earlier reviews, identities,
announcements and dates remain unchanged. This cohort brings the source
inventory to 965 reviews, 165 expansion additions and 28 guides; 36 more
distinct source reviews remain before the requested 1,001 target.

## Original sources and inference limits

Twenty-three original references were checked directly. Most are NOAA/NWS,
JetStream, NESDIS or CPC educational and measurement explanations with shared
agency provenance. NSIDC supplies a separate scientific explanation, not an
independent expert poll or a new app-validation experiment. The precipitation
page identifies NASA Space Place/JPL authorship, NOAA program funding and
August 27, 2025 publication. Undated pages receive no fabricated year.

Sources retain the technical-reference category, no formal appraisal, no coded
study design and no expert-review identity. Legacy confidence is editorial,
not the proportion of meteorologists agreeing or the fraction of forecasts
correct. No current observations, weather-app performance, private requests,
historical empirical percentages, local crop/road safety, medical diagnosis or
safe-exposure times were measured or inferred. Conceptual distributions and
cooling examples are explicitly illustrative, not observed weather outcomes.

NWS forecast conventions and U.S. ice-pellet usage of sleet are identified;
they are not attributed to every country or app. Historical grid dimensions,
clock conventions, warning criteria, uncited snow averages, example model
distributions, radar installations and palettes are not universal current
product facts. Snow diagrams illustrate sufficient profiles rather than
proving that an above-freezing surface rules out arrival. Snow arrival and
accumulation are separate. Dry-object convection excludes neither radiative
nor evaporative cooling. WBGT differs from ordinary wet-bulb temperature.
Station pressure, sea-level reduction and upper-air heights retain their
distinct datums. Nineteenth-century forecasting folklore quoted by NOAA is
not adopted. Typical frontal precipitation and motion are not guarantees.

Two Consensus.app searches and one fetched record supported communication
discovery: Gigerenzer and colleagues' 2005 rain-probability paper. The original
MPG repository retrieval returned 403; its generated takeaway and historical
survey findings are not canonical evidence. No Deep Review was used, and no
remaining account quota is asserted. A current NDFD page retrieval also failed;
accessible original NWS definitions supply the product scope instead. The
legacy GOES product link redirected to a general satellite page, and the
specific VLab cloud-top page was unavailable. Neither is claimed as verified
cloud-top evidence. Accessible NESDIS/CPC explanations supply the radiance and
non-precipitating-cloud limits. A third-party frost tutorial hosted by NWS was
not adopted as an original institutional reference.

## Acceptance boundary

Tests use actual schemas, stable announcements/dates, complete canonical path
checks, diagnostic search, intentional collection membership and mathematical
counterexamples. Shared generic tests preserve the baseline 800 records and
earlier source metadata. Guide checks require substantive original paragraphs,
traceable non-decorative citations, lazy loading and honest unsupported answers.
Native install/parity/lint/typecheck/test/build and full isolated browser,
account, editorial, roadmap/following/update and legacy-citation checks remain
required before source delivery. The frozen unfamiliar-query evaluation stays
unopened until final-library acceptance and is not used to select these gaps.

Source seed status is not production publication. Startup seeding remains
disabled in production. No deployment, production access, credentials,
database mutation or authenticated editorial publication is authorized by
this source cohort. Current official local forecasts and warnings are needed
for hazardous weather; this library does not replace them. Whole-library
1000+ wording remains gated on independent actual public coverage. Protected
public publication, real approved-roadmap/following acceptance and the final
fresh usefulness evaluation remain unfinished parts of the active full goal.

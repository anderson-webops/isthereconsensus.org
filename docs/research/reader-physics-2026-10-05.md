## Qualifications preserved

- Heat-capacity approximations are not universal absolute-energy formulas.
- Phase plateaus assume the stated pure-substance equilibrium and pressure.
- Contact sensation involves transient exchange, not conductivity alone or safe-touch certification.
- A vacuum removes gas transfer, not radiative exchange; gross and net exchange differ.
- Infrared radiance-to-temperature conversion depends on the target and model, not display precision.
- Prism dispersion is not every nonlinear or absorbing light-matter process.
- The rainbow diagram's total-internal-reflection label is not applied to every rainbow ray.
- Real/virtual object, surrounding medium and single versus compound optics remain explicit.
- Rayleigh's ordinary imaging criterion is not an unrestricted limit on specialized techniques.
- Phase, group and information velocities are distinguished without faster-than-light messaging claims.
- Polarization does not certify UV protection or eye safety.
- Simplified textbook greenhouse temperatures/feedback and NASA solar peak-color/flame passages are not used as new climate or thermometer evidence.
